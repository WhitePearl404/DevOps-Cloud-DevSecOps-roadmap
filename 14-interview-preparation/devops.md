# DevOps Interview Questions

## Fundamentals

1. What is DevOps and what problems does it solve?
2. CI vs CD vs continuous deployment?
3. What makes a good CI/CD pipeline?
4. What is infrastructure automation?
5. Immutable infrastructure vs mutable infrastructure?
6. What is configuration drift?
7. Blue/green vs canary deployment?
8. How do you design rollback?
9. How should secrets be handled in CI/CD?
10. How do you make deployments auditable?

## Scenario questions

11. A deployment passes CI but fails in production. How do you investigate?
12. A pipeline takes 40 minutes. How would you reduce it?
13. A deployment partially succeeds. How do you recover?
14. A developer wants production credentials in GitHub Actions. What do you propose instead?
15. How would you design CI/CD for multiple environments?

## Follow-up prompts

For each answer, expect:

- Why?
- What are the trade-offs?
- What can fail?
- How do you secure it?
- How do you monitor it?
- What did you implement personally?
